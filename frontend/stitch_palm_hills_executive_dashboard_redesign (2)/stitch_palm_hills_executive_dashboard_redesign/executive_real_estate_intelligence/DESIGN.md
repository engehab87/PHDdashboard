## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an architectural, executive-grade operational environment engineered for senior leadership managing high-end integrated communities, asset portfolios, security operations, and vehicle intelligence. The aesthetic embodies prestige real estate development: restrained luxury, rigorous precision, and clinical clarity without digital noise.

### Design Principles
- **Architectural Minimalism:** Layouts prioritize spatial balance, structural alignment, and subtle warmth through limestone and alabaster tonal shifts rather than gratuitous embellishment.
- **Selective Crimson Accents:** Echoing the iconic geometric brand identity, crimson/ruby red serves strictly as an intentional focal point for primary actions, critical alerts, and brand anchors. It is never diluted through arbitrary decorative saturation.
- **Analytical Rigor:** Information architecture is organized for high-density legibility, allowing directors to scan security checkpoints, gate access records, facility statuses, and operational incidents seamlessly.
- **Tactile Material Quality:** Elements utilize hairline structural dividers, crisp geometric silhouettes, and subtle ambient warmth to reflect the physical quality of premium built environments.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the warm architectural neutrals of limestone, bone, and off-white plaster, contrasted against deep charcoal ink and punctuated by signature crimson.

### Color Tokens & Usage
- **Primary (`#C4161C`):** Used strictly for high-impact interactive states, primary conversion controls, active navigation anchors, and executive summary callouts.
- **Secondary (`#1A1A1A`):** Defines high-contrast typography, structural dark surfaces, and secondary control elements requiring executive gravitas.
- **Tertiary (`#9E1218`):** Deep burgundy-red dedicated to active/pressed states, high-priority alert badges, and urgent operational notices.
- **Surfaces (`#FBFBF9`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F5F5F3`):** Canvas backgrounds employ warm alabaster (`#FBFBF9`), elevated cards utilize pristine white (`#FFFFFF`), and nested headers or dense table rows utilize bone gray (`#F5F5F3`).
- **Dividers (`#EAEAE7`, `#D5D5D0`):** Ultra-fine hairline structural strokes that establish clear data division without visual clutter.
- **Internal Non-Blue Links:** Hyperlinks and records within data views strictly use deep charcoal (`#181818`) with an underline hover state transitioning smoothly into crimson (`#C4161C`).

## Typography

The typography pairs the geometric authority of `Montserrat` for headlines and architectural metadata with the modern, open legibility of `Outfit` for dense analytical tables, forms, and operational flows.

### Typographic Rules
- **Headline Architecture:** Major headers and metric callouts leverage `Montserrat` with controlled letter-spacing, mirroring the bold geometric aesthetic of high-end architectural signage.
- **Uppercase Metadata:** Status tags, table headers, and category super-headers utilize `label-caps` (`Montserrat`, bold, 11px, `letter-spacing: 0.12em`) rendered in all uppercase to instill institutional authority.
- **Tabular Data:** Numeric values in operational grids and telemetry readouts require tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment across dense balance and flow sheets.
- **Link Treatments:** Non-branded system links maintain standard body weight and charcoal color, deploying an instantaneous color shift to red with an underline upon interaction.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a fluid 12-column grid optimized for dense, multi-pane executive operations.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with 24px gutters and 40px outer margins. Supports side-by-side surveillance streams, complex filter sidebars, and multi-column telemetry tables.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns with 20px gutters and 24px outer margins. Secondary operational cards stack into double-column modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px gutters and 16px outer margins. Data tables convert to collapsible summary cards.

### Density & Cadence
- **Executive Data Density:** Table rows adhere to a tight 44px height profile in standard view and 36px in compact surveillance mode, maximizing screen real estate for incident monitoring.
- **Metric Cards:** Executive KPIs use `space-lg` (24px) internal padding, anchoring primary metric numbers next to micro-trend visualizers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through subtle tonal separation, refined hairline borders, and whisper-quiet ambient shadows that mimic natural architectural light.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Surfaces sit on `#FBFBF9`. No shadow.
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Cards):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px solid `#EAEAE7` outline. Ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(24, 24, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Flyouts & Dropdowns):** Layered above interactive tables. Pure `#FFFFFF` with border `#D5D5D0`. Ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(24, 24, 24, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Critical Incident Modals):** Pure `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px solid `#D5D5D0` with top 3px crimson brand rule (`#C4161C`). Ambient shadow: `0 16px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a soft architectural geometry (`roundedness: 1`), utilizing 4px (`0.25rem`) corners for core controls and inputs, 8px (`0.5rem`) for container panels, and 12px (`0.75rem`) for modal dialogs.

### Shape Details
- **Buttons and Inputs:** 4px radius ensures crisp alignment with high-density data matrices while remaining approachable.
- **Status Badges & Chips:** Minimal 4px radius or rectangular pill styling, avoiding bubbly forms in favor of precision.
- **Diamond Icon Element:** The signature Palm Hills 4-diamond motif is incorporated into loading states, active tab markers, and breadcrumb separators as a crisp, unrounded 45-degree geometric icon.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with signature Crimson (`#C4161C`), white text, 4px border radius, 40px height for standard actions. Hover: `#9E1218`. Active: `#820E13`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#D5D5D0` border and `#181818` text. Hover: `#F5F5F3` with `#181818` border.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `#181818` text. Hover: `#F5F5F3` background with `#C4161C` text.

### Data Tables (Executive & Security Records)
- **Header:** Background `#F5F5F3`, border-bottom 1px `#D5D5D0`, text `label-caps` in `#666666`.
- **Rows:** Background `#FFFFFF` alternating with clean borders (`#EAEAE7`). Hover row state: `#FBFBF9`.
- **Cells:** Vertical padding of 10px, horizontal padding of 16px. Numerical data and vehicle IDs right-aligned or monospaced/tabular.
- **Internal Links:** Dark charcoal (`#181818`), medium weight; transitions to `#C4161C` on hover with a crisp 1px underline offset.

### Status Badges & Indicators
- **Operational Normal:** Border 1px `#D4EDDA`, background `#F2F9F4`, text `#1E7E34`.
- **Under Review / Caution:** Border 1px `#FFF3CD`, background `#FDFBF2`, text `#856404`.
- **Security Alert / Flagged:** Border 1px `#F8D7DA`, background `#FDF3F3`, text `#B81D24`. Accompanied by a 6px solid diamond indicator.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#D5D5D0` border, 4px border radius, 40px height.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#C4161C` border with an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(196, 22, 28, 0.1)` halo.
- **Prefix Icons:** Muted charcoal (`#666666`) architectural line icons (magnifier, gate marker, vehicle silhouette).

### Executive KPI Metric Cards
- **Structure:** White card `#FFFFFF` on `#FBFBF9` canvas, 1px `#EAEAE7` border, 24px padding.
- **Content Flow:** Upper row displays uppercase micro-label (`label-caps`) with optional diamond accent; middle row features large display numeral (`display-lg`); lower row provides trend micro-copy with subtle green/red telemetry tags.
